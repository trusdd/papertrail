# papertrail

Local document organizer with OCR search. Drop your PDFs and scans into a folder — find anything inside them instantly, without sending a single byte to the cloud.

## Why

Your receipts, contracts, warranties, and medical records are scattered across folders named "Downloads" and "Desktop". papertrail turns that chaos into a searchable, private archive — like Google Photos, but for your papers.

## Features

- Local OCR (Russian and English)
- SQLite index stored next to your documents
- Full-text search inside PDFs and images
- Portable archive — copy the folder, keep everything
- No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry
